## Planning notes

- Suggestions are alternatives. Choose one per gap and rerun after accepting; a customer may appear in several alternative gaps.
- No customer messages are sent. No default service interval is invented.
- Travel times are estimates. All times use the buyer-selected local timezone; overnight shifts must be split into date rows.
- Unassigned heuristic results are not proofs that no feasible schedule exists.
- Planning period: 2026-10-19 through 2026-10-19; local timezone: Europe/Berlin.
- Backfill search targets the 12 nearest eligible customers per gap for road checks; already-known road pairs can cover additional customers. It does not guarantee exhaustive coverage or the globally best backfill.
- Addresses/coordinates are processed by Geoapify. Customer names, contact details and service history are not sent to the map provider.
